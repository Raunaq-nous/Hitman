1Summary

Hitman is an IPL-only fantasy app built on Next.js. Its fantasy points are generated at random, its prize pool runs through a personal UPI ID, and its admin routes are open to anyone. India’s 2025 online gaming law prohibits money games whether they rely on skill or chance, and bans facilitating payments for them, so the payment flow has to come down before anything else.2627

The market has moved since the build. Leagues and big tech now ship AI companions, AI-voiced recaps and AI-derived stats: the Premier League’s Copilot companion, ESPN’s personalized SportsCenter, the NBA’s new tracking metrics and Wimbledon’s Match Chat.12141618 A collated summary of what’s happening is now table stakes, not a product.

The open space is depth across sports plus a game that scores fans on how well they read the game. Every insight carries a receipt back to its source data, and every call is graded on the quality of the decision rather than the outcome. The legends’ nicknames give the product its story, but HITMAN and Mamba are protected marks, so the public product uses its own names.

Decisions locked in this version

The working title is ALTER EGO, pending a trademark search. Five persona agents carry public names: The Opener for cricket, The Closer for basketball, The Architect for football, The Professor for F1 and The Iceman for tennis. The legends’ codenames stay internal and appear only as editorial lineage.

Make the Call is the signature mechanic, graded on decision quality. There is no real money anywhere in the product. Cricket and F1 come first because their open data is the deepest.

Open the landing concept, which includes a working three-call demo.

2The codebase today

The repository (package name pitch11-app) is a Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind 4 and Prisma application deployed to Vercel as a progressive web app. It runs to about 5,300 lines across 40 source files. It offers credential login, private leagues joined by invite code, an 11-player team builder with credits and role limits, a hand-typed IPL 2026 schedule and points table, live scores from CricketData.org’s current-matches endpoint, a template-driven ball-by-ball simulator, leaderboards and a “Treasurer” prize pool. There is no language model, retrieval or agent anywhere in the code.

Worth keeping

The Next.js 16, React 19 and Tailwind 4 shell is current. The PWA setup works, the league-invite flow is a good social loop, and a seeded simulator is the right idea for deterministic replays once it’s calibrated on real ball-by-ball data.

4What changed in AI

An agent is now described as a model plus a harness: the loop, tools, context management, safety controls and orchestration that sit around the model. Harness engineering was named as a discipline in early 2026.49 Practitioners frame it as the third phase of AI engineering, after prompt engineering and context engineering, with the focus moving to autonomy, accuracy and control.50

A source-code study of eleven production coding harnesses found that SKILL.md skills had overtaken MCP in adoption, nine systems against eight, and that the harness turned from a tool into a platform during the first half of 2026.49 The working discipline is to treat agent mistakes as permanent signals, so every rule in an AGENTS.md file traces back to something that actually went wrong.51

Anthropic’s guidance for long-running work pairs an initializer agent, which sets up the environment, with coding agents that make incremental progress and leave artifacts such as a progress log for the next session.52 Its Managed Agents service separates the session log, the harness loop and the sandbox, so long-horizon work doesn’t depend on any one context window.53 Related engineering notes cover context engineering, Agent Skills and code execution with MCP.54

For ALTER EGO, two harnesses matter. The build harness is how you and your coding agents build the product. The runtime harness is how the product itself reasons about sport. Section 5 specifies both.

5Target architecture

Runtime harness

Each layer hands a narrower, better-checked object to the next. Language models only ever reason over numbers that the layers below have computed and can trace.

  1. SourcesLicensed live feeds, plus open data: Cricsheet, FastF1, Jolpica and OpenF1, StatsBomb open data, nba_api and tennis point-by-point files.
  2. AdaptersOne MCP server per data domain. Each normalizes into the canonical model, rate-limits, caches and stamps attribution.
  3. Event storeAppend-only incidents (deliveries, laps, possessions, points) in Postgres, with Redis for live fan-out and SSE or WebSockets to clients.
  4. ModelsPlain code: win probability per sport, ball-by-ball and race simulators, the swing index and the decision grader.
  5. AgentsAn orchestrator plus the five roster agents, each with a SKILL.md playbook. Models reason and narrate over computed outputs.
  6. GuardsA provenance check that every number resolves to a record, a numeric fact-checker, golden-question evals per sport, and policy checks for real-money language and impersonation.
  7. SurfacesThe Desk, Make the Call, the Roster and Alter Ego, plus an MCP endpoint so assistants can call the analysis.

Canonical data model

Sport → Competition → Season → Event
Event → Participants (teams, players, drivers)
Event → Periods (innings, halves, quarters, sets, stints)
Period → Incidents (delivery, possession, lap, point)
         { t, actor, type, payload, source_ref }
Incident → Derived (win_prob, swing, xG, xFG, tyre_age, ...)
Call → { moment_ref, options[], model_read, pick, grade, locked_at }

The source_ref on every incident is what makes receipts possible: any sentence the product writes must be able to cite the incidents it relied on. Borrowing field names from an existing sports-data standard will make provider adapters easier to write.

Build harness

Start with a real AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md that map the architecture, the canonical model, the commands and the never-do rules, such as “never fabricate a score” and “never add a payment path”. Add hooks that block merges without a passing typecheck, lint, tests and a secret scan. Give each data adapter its own sub-agent and fixture set of golden match data, so tests act as the agent’s sensors. Keep a progress file per epic so long builds survive across sessions.

6The market

How fans watch now

Younger fans increasingly follow sport through highlights and clips. In a June 2026 survey, 47% of 18 to 24-year-olds and 51% of 25 to 34-year-olds said highlights were replacing live games for them, against 38% of all Americans. 58% of both groups use a second screen during live sport, and more than half want one app for every sport they watch.5 A YouGov-based study of Gen Z found 92% of Gen Z sports fans do something else on their phones while a game is on.6 On the industry side, 81% of 675 sports media executives said they had expanded their use of AI in the past year, and they expect owned apps and social video to overtake websites as the main fan channels by 2030.82

Where the money is going

2025 was a record year, with about $200B in announced sports-tech deal value across more than 1,000 deals.1 Q1 2026 added $130.6B across 239 deals, including $2.9B of private financing over 117 rounds, 80% of them early stage. The largest rounds went to prediction markets and wearables: Kalshi raised about $1B at a valuation near $22B, Polymarket took $600M from ICE, WHOOP raised $575M and Novig $75M.2 Fan engagement and experience made up a third of sports-tech M&A value in 2024,83 and fan-platform startups now crowd the seed-to-Series A market.4

India specifics

A Google and Deloitte report counts 655 million sports fans in India, 36% of them women and 59% in rural areas.7 English accounted for under 10% of cricket watch time on JioStar in the latest season, while regional languages such as Haryanvi and Bhojpuri grew about 300% a year.8 Women made up 47% of IPL viewers on Star Sports in 2025, and JioStar shipped AI highlights within minutes of the final ball and real-time AI translation of expert commentary.9 For IPL 2026 it added ChatGPT for live multilingual questions,8 after a February 2026 partnership with OpenAI that also surfaces JioHotstar content inside ChatGPT.10 IPL franchise values hit records, with RCB at about $1.78B and RR at about $1.63B.2

The constraint that shapes the game

India’s Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, passed on 21 August 2025, prohibits online money games whether they rely on skill or chance, and prohibits offering, advertising or facilitating payments for them. Offering one can mean up to three years in prison and a ₹1 crore fine.2627 Dream11 paused its paid contests, which had been more than 90% of its revenue.27 ALTER EGO is therefore free to play by design. Any physical prizes need advice from Indian counsel first; this page is not legal advice.

11Product story

Positioning

For fans who follow more than one sport, ALTER EGO is the sports desk and game that turns watching into reading the game. It gives you one brief ranked by what mattered, live calls graded on decision quality, and specialist analysts built around legendary mentalities. Unlike league apps and AI recaps, it works across sports, shows a receipt for every number, and lets you earn an identity for how you read the game.

The story

Nicknames in sport are earned, not chosen. A Star Sports crew member called Rohit Sharma “the Hitman” after his first ODI double hundred in Bengaluru in 2013, and Ravi Shastri carried it onto the air.6185 The innings was 209 off 158 balls with 16 sixes.62 Kobe Bryant built the Black Mamba as a way of working, not just a name. ALTER EGO gives fans the same arc: read the game well, repeatedly, and the product names you for how you do it.

The game loop

A moment is frozen, the fan picks an option and locks it, and the reveal shows how the model read each option. The grade is the difference in model-estimated win chance between the option chosen and the alternative at the time of the call. The outcome is shown but never changes the grade, which keeps the game about skill and makes a lucky result worth nothing. Grades feed an Elo-style Read Rating, and patterns in a fan’s calls feed the Alter Ego.

Two design constraints matter. Calls must close on data-feed time, before any stream shows the result, because broadcast delays vary by platform. And the model’s read must be calibrated, so it’s published with its track record; if fans can beat it consistently, they should see that too.

Revenue

A freemium subscription unlocks deeper roster analysis, replays and full call history. Brands can sponsor moments rather than odds, and the decision-game engine can be licensed to rights holders who want interactive moments in their own apps. There are no entry fees, no payouts and no betting partnerships.

What we won’t build

No real-money contests, no highlight clipping, no cloned voices or likenesses of real people, and no stats scraped without a licence.

12Fan journeys

The multi-sport follower

Follows the IPL, F1 and the Premier League. Opens the Desk with morning coffee and sees one ranked brief instead of three apps. Makes a bowling-change call during an evening chase and a pit-window call on Sunday. Their file shows they’re sharper at F1 strategy than at cricket captaincy.

The analyst

Lives on the coach setting. Taps receipts to check every claim, runs what-ifs in Pit Wall and Blueprint, and shares receipt cards in group chats. The Read Rating is their reputation, and they want to beat the model.

The second-screen fan

Watches clips more than full games and keeps the phone in hand during big matches. Reads a three-minute brief in Hindi, makes two quick calls during a final, and shares the alter-ego reveal. For them, the product is the reason to watch the moment live.

Why the public names differ

HITMAN is a registered US trademark of IO Interactive for computer and video game software, renewed in 2015,56 and related HITMAN marks cover online games.57 Kobe Bryant’s estate has filed Mamba, Mamba League and the Mamba logo,58 extended its marks to digital goods including avatars and trading cards,59 and protects the brand closely.60

The agents therefore embody a mentality and never impersonate the athlete: no cloned voices, no likeness and no implied endorsement. Lineage copy stays factual and editorial. Run a trademark search on ALTER EGO and the persona names, and have counsel review the lineage copy before public launch.

Motion is limited to one orchestrated moment, the poster wall rising on load, plus responses to the visitor’s own actions such as the reveal.
