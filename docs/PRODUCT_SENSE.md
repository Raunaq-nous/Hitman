# Product sense

## Thesis
There is a game inside every game. ALTER EGO rewards fans for becoming more perceptive, not for being lucky: you make a call before the outcome, we grade the **decision**, and you learn something you can use on the next one.

## The loop
Discover → Understand → Decide → Commit → Watch → Review → Learn → Return.

## North star
**Review-to-Next-Read rate** = users who review a resolved decision and then make another ÷ users with a resolved decision (per week).

## Supporting KPIs (targets are hypotheses to test)
Graded calls per weekly active fan in an IPL week ≥ 8 · call participation ≥ 35% · new-fan first-call rate ≥ 60% · D7/D30 retention 30%/15% · share rate per Alter Ego reveal ≥ 20% · receipt tap-through ≥ 5% · fact-checker pass rate 100% · calibration bucket error ≤ 5 points · feed-to-screen latency ≤ 2 s (when live exists).
Four scoreboards: Trust (freshness, corrections, leakage), Product (conversion, completion, Review-to-Next-Read), Learning (calibration, comprehension), Business (ARPU, data cost per user, model cost per read).

## The four pillars
1. **The Desk** — a cross-sport brief ranked by swing (how far win probability moved), a receipt on every number, a depth switch (Quick / Tactical / Analyst), English and Hindi, Event Rooms for five sports.
2. **Make the Call / Decision Lab** — a pre-outcome decision with confidence (50–99%), locked at feed time, graded on counterfactual win probability, reviewed in three columns, scored with Brier calibration and a Read Rating. Historical first; live only after a licensed feed.
3. **The Roster** — five original analyst characters with skill words and playbooks.
4. **Alter Ego and the Notebook** — an identity earned from your calling style, calibration charts, cross-sport skill domains, a Season File.

## Principles
- Grade the decision, not the outcome. A good read that lost is still a good read.
- Honest uncertainty: when options are within the coin-flip band, say “coin flip — no grade”.
- Receipts over vibes: every number links to its source and time.
- Calm, dense, legible: designed like a dossier, not a casino.
- India-first: Hindi at launch; cricket depth first.

## Legal frame (summary)
India’s Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 bans online money games (in force from 1 October 2025; offering one can mean up to three years in prison, a fine up to ₹1 crore, or both). ALTER EGO is a free, no-stakes product: no deposits, entry fees, cash, prizes, vouchers or rewards of value — not even from sponsors — without a written legal opinion. Personality rights are actively enforced by Indian courts (for example, the Delhi High Court’s July 2026 interim order protecting Yuvraj Singh’s name, image, voice and likeness, including against AI deepfakes).

## What is copy and what is novel
Copy (don’t compete on it): AI chat over archives, live win-probability graphs, AI highlights, personalised multi-sport feeds, fantasy XIs.
Novel (our moat): pre-outcome decisions graded on counterfactual win probability; cross-sport swing on one scale with receipts; a difficulty-weighted Read Rating for decisions; the three-column Review; an identity from calling style; a personal decision Notebook with calibration.

## Not now
Live calls (until licensed), money of any kind, cloned voices, real athletes as characters, computer-vision tracking (V3, creator tools only), building our own cameras or feeds.
