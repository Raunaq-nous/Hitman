# Plans

- Small changes: a few lines in `harness/progress.md`.
- Large work: an execution plan in `docs/exec-plans/active/` with Purpose, Phases and Gates (checkboxes), Decision Log (dated, with reasoning), Progress Log, Surprises & Discoveries, and Outcomes. Plans are versioned with the code and moved to `completed/` when done.
- Every decision that a human might have made is logged with the options considered and why one was chosen.
