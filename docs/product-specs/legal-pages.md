# Legal and trust pages

- `/terms`: free to play; no money, entry fees, prizes or rewards of value; acceptable use; beta 18+.
- `/privacy`: what is stored (account, calls, ratings, crews, analytics events), why, retention, export and deletion.
- `/attributions`: every data source with its attribution text and licence notes (from `src/rights/policies`).
- `/ai`: how personas work, that they are AI, how receipts and the claim verifier work, how to report an error.
- Account settings: export my data (JSON), delete my account (hard delete with confirmation).
All pages are drafts for counsel; `docs/HUMAN_TODO.md` tracks legal review.
